GOVERNANCE & CODE OF CONDUCT

Terms of Service & Rules.

Last Updated: August 17, 2026 · Mandatory for all active athletes & guests.

1. Agreement to Terms

By entering Namma Gym facilities, purchasing a trial pass, or accessing our digital member portal, you agree to comply with these terms, health safety guidelines, and code of athletic conduct.

2. Physical Readiness & Liability Waiver

Strength training, powerlifting, and combat sports involve inherent physical exertion. Members confirm they are medically fit to engage in strenuous exercise and release Namma Gym Pvt. Ltd. from liability for injuries arising from standard training hazards.

3. Iron Floor & Hardware Etiquette

  • Re-racking: All weight plates, dumbbells, and kettlebells must be returned to their designated trees after use.
  • Chalk: Liquid and block chalk are permitted on Olympic platforms; please wipe down barbell knurling after use.
  • Footwear: Clean training shoes or lifting shoes required. Barefoot training allowed strictly on tatami mats.

4. Combat Octagon & Sparring Protocol

Live sparring is permitted strictly during coach-supervised slots. Mandatory protective equipment (16oz gloves, mouthguard, shin pads) is required. Unsanctioned or aggressive sparring without coach oversight will result in immediate membership termination.

5. Thermal Contrast Suite Guidelines

Members must shower before entering the cold plunge pool or cedar dry sauna. Maximum recommended duration in the 3°C plunge is 3 minutes per cycle. Individuals with cardiovascular conditions must consult their physician.

6. Cancellation & Freeze Terms

Recurring monthly memberships can be cancelled anytime with 0 fees prior to the auto-renew date. Quarterly and Annual memberships can be placed on medical or travel freeze for up to 30 days per year via the Member Portal.
