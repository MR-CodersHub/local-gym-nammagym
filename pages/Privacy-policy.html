LEGAL & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: August 17, 2026 · Effective for all Namma Gym members & website users.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for a membership, book a trial pass, or subscribe to our training dispatch, we collect:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, phone number, email address, emergency contact.
  • Billing Information: Transaction IDs, UPI handles, and payment gateway references (we do not store raw card numbers).
  • Physical Baselines: InBody 570 scan metrics (weight, skeletal muscle mass, body fat %) if voluntary.

2. Health & Biometric Data Safety

Health assessments and physical screening data conducted during 1-on-1 coaching or membership onboarding are stored encrypted in compliance with Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) standards. Your health records are accessible only to your assigned CSCS head coach.

3. RFID Gate Access & Attendance

We log check-in timestamps via your digital member QR code and RFID gate keycards for facility safety, occupancy monitoring, and automated attendance streak tracking inside your Member Portal.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Marketing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared exclusively with critical infrastructure partners (payment processors like Razorpay, and WhatsApp messaging for class schedule updates).

5. Member Rights & Data Deletion

You retain full authority to review, export, or permanently delete your membership profile upon account closure by emailing our Data Protection Officer at privacy@nammagym.in.
